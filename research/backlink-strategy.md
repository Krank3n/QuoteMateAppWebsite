# Backlink strategy — authentic link building for quotemate.app

Drafted 2026-07-15/16. Status of each workstream at the bottom.

## Core principle

Comment/forum links are nearly always nofollow — their value is referral
traffic, brand searches, and repeated visibility in tradie communities, not
direct SEO. So the strategy splits: (A) links that move rankings, (B)
community presence done for humans.

**Standing rule (2026-07-16):** on Reddit, comments are 100% useful
information — no product mentions, no links. Attribution via profile bio
("Founder of QuoteMate — quoting app for AU tradies"). Product mentions only
where platform-sanctioned: Quora (disclosed), Flying Solo (signature link).
See memory: feedback-reddit-value-only.

## A. Links that move rankings

1. **Integration marketplaces (first — high authority, zero outreach)**
   - Xero App Store listing — copy drafted: store-listing/xero-app-store-listing.md.
     Requires joining Xero App Partner Program + certification (OAuth already
     compliant). Support URL placeholder needs confirming.
   - Square App Marketplace / partner directory — same logic, not yet drafted.

2. **Software directories** — Capterra, GetApp, Software Advice, G2, Product
   Hunt launch, SoftwareSuggest, AU directories (Flying Solo tools lists).
   One afternoon of profile setup, permanent links.

3. **Digital PR with original data (highest ceiling)** — annual "Australian
   Trade Quoting Report": average quote values by trade, turnaround times,
   acceptance rates (anonymised/aggregated; supplement with a surveyed panel
   via Facebook groups + Bunnings-voucher draw if user base too small).
   Pitch: trade media (Inside Construction, Build Australia, Plumbing
   Connection, Electrical Connection, HVAC&R News) + small-biz press
   (SmartCompany, Kochie's Business Builders, Flying Solo). Ongoing:
   journalist callouts via SourceBottle (AU), Qwoted, Featured.

4. **Guest content + podcasts** — tradie business podcasts (The Site Shed,
   Tradie Success, The Tradie Show, Small Business Big Marketing): founder
   story + "what 1,000 quotes taught us". Guest posts for
   accountants/bookkeepers serving tradies, TAFE resource pages, supplier
   blogs. Overlaps partnerships-bd lane.

5. **Template pages as link magnets** — outreach to articles ranking for
   "how to quote [trade] jobs" that lack a template; offer the relevant
   /templates/ page as a referenced resource.

## B. Community commenting (value-only)

Full playbook + 51 verified threads: research/comment-engagement-targets.md.
Drafts for top 5: research/comment-drafts-top5.md.

Rules recap: real identity, answer completely in the comment, 5–8
threads/week (~1–2 hrs), work the priority queue top-down. Reddit account
needs karma/agr before it's useful — start with no-link value comments.

Platform notes: Flying Solo = best AU forum (signature link sanctioned);
Whirlpool = advice-only, strict (ACCC-citing anti-astroturf rules); Homeone =
assume no promo; dead ends = Renovate Forum (shut 2023), ProductReview
(affiliation rules), Bunnings Workshop (homeowner audience).

## What to skip

Paid link packages, Fiverr links, PBNs, automated comment tools, reciprocal
swaps. No free quote-generator linkbait (cannibalises the paid app — standing
rule).

### Findings that change the plan

- **Template outreach (A5) has a thin target pool.** Of the AU pages ranking
  for "how to quote [trade]", Built Simple, FlowSpec, DayRoute, NextMinute and
  AroFlo are software vendors; The Quote Yard and Tradies Club are lead-gen
  marketplaces; BizCover is an insurer's 2022 marketing post with no external
  links. The one independent publication is **Blue Collar News**
  (hello@bluecollarnews.com.au, bylined, accepts tips). Treat A5 as folded
  into A3 (trade media) rather than a separate workstream.
- **Bookkeepers for tradies are the better A4 route.** SmartDigits
  (Melbourne, names ServiceM8 and Tradify in content), Tradies Bookkeeping
  (Sydney, Xero), Bookkept, Double Balance, Future Advisory, Walker Hill all
  publish tradie content and already link to tools. That is a partnerships
  conversation (co-marketing, referral) that ends in a link, not a media
  pitch. Hand to the partnerships lane.
- **Associations are member-gated.** Master Plumbers WA publishes a Pricing
  and Estimating Guide and a charge-out calculator for members; Master
  Electricians and NECA gate resources. Low odds for a link; possible for a
  supplier-directory listing later.
- **Reece is not an outreach target for links** (it ranks us for
  `reece max login`), but it is a partner with a live badge: a listing in
  Reece's own partner/app directory, if one exists, is worth one email.

### Measure

The only honest measure is the GA referral report by `sessionSource`, 90-day
window, re-run monthly, looking for any source that is not self-owned, a
product surface or a search engine. Today that list is DevHunt and Product
Hunt. Search Console's Links report (needs the service account on the
property) will show the acquired links themselves.
