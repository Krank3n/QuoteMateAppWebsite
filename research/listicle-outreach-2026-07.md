build "best quoting app for Australian tradies" responses. All three omit
QuoteMate. I fetched each one to confirm who runs it and what motivates them,
because the right pitch is different in all three cases — and sending the
wrong one (money to the editorial site, editorial appeals to the affiliate
site) burns the contact.

## 1. TradieScaler — commercial pitch

**Target article:** Best Free Quoting Software for Australian Tradies (2026)
`https://tradiescaler.com/quoting-estimating/free-quoting-software-tradies/`

**Why they're first:** their disclosure says plainly, *"Tradie Scaler earns a
commission when you sign up via our links. This doesn't affect our
rankings."* The incentive is explicit and we can meet it. Better still, the
article is specifically about **free** quoting software — and QuoteMate has a
genuine free-forever plan, not a trial dressed up as one. That's a real
editorial fit, not a stretch.

**Contact:** no email published — use the contact form, and check `/about/`
and `/disclaimer/` for a direct address first.

**Before sending:** decide the commission. There's no affiliate program
today, so offer a manual arrangement you can honour by hand — a flat
per-paying-signup bounty is simpler to track than a revenue share at this
volume, and doesn't require building anything.

>

>

> pitch, but it fits the brief you set: free forever, unlimited quotes and

>

actively maintained, so an update is realistic rather than a rewrite request.

**Critical:** the article states *"No affiliate rankings. No sponsored
placements."* Do not offer money, commission, or a link exchange. That single
mistake ends this one permanently. ServiceScale sells websites and AI
coaching for tradies — complementary to us, not competing, so there's no
conflict in them listing us.

## Follow-up

One follow-up only, 7–9 days later, then drop it. A second chase on a cold
pitch converts almost nothing and it's how a sender reputation gets built.

> **Subject:** Re: [original subject]
>
> Hi — just bumping this once in case it got buried. No problem at all if
> it's not a fit; I won't chase it further.
>
> The offer of a free account stands either way if you ever want to look at
> it.

> Tom

## What to expect

Realistically one of the three converts. TradieScaler is the most likely
because the incentive is stated and the free-plan fit is real. ServiceScale
is the most *valuable* if it lands — an unpaid editorial mention on a site
that publicly refuses sponsorship is exactly the corroboration answer engines
weight most, and it's the one that can't be bought.

Track replies. If TradieScaler converts, the same commercial pitch scales to
the wider AU affiliate-publisher set, and that becomes a repeatable channel
rather than three one-off emails.
