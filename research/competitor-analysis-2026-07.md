# QuoteMate — Competitive Landscape Analysis

**Prepared:** July 2026 · **Market:** Australia (sole-trader & small-crew tradies) · **Scope:** 40+ direct, adjacent, and emerging competitors across 7 categories.

> **How to read this doc.** All pricing is AUD unless marked USD, and reflects what was published/reported mid-2026 — SaaS pricing and promos change constantly, and several vendors (simPRO, ServiceTitan, FieldPulse, AroFlo, Databuild) publish **no** public pricing, so those figures are reseller/review estimates. Items I couldn't confirm to a primary source are flagged **[verify]**. Each competitor gets an honest "where QuoteMate wins / where it genuinely loses" read — this is a strategy tool, not marketing copy.

## 1. Executive summary

QuoteMate competes in one of the most crowded software niches in Australia — tradie quoting/invoicing/job-management — but occupies a **specific, defensible slot**: *AI-native, mobile-first, live-supplier-priced quoting for the sole trader and small crew, at flat pricing with a free tier.* No single competitor occupies exactly that slot, but QuoteMate is squeezed from several directions at once.

**The five things that matter most:**

1. **QuoteMate's two real moats are (a) genuinely live, named Australian supplier pricing (Bunnings / Reece / Tradelink) and (b) in-app getting-paid via Square tap-to-pay + PayID/BPAY.** Almost nobody combines both. Lead every comparison with these — the AI-quoting angle itself is *no longer differentiating* (see below).

2. **AI quoting is being commoditised in real time.** In ~18 months the "describe a job → 30-second priced quote" pitch went from empty to crowded: cheap AU/NZ clones (**QuotesYa** $19–35/mo, **Insta Quotes** $29–99/mo) copy it almost word-for-word and undercut on price, a funded US category leader (**Handoff/1build**, ~US$25M raised) proves the thesis attracts serious capital, and incumbents (**ServiceM8, AroFlo, Jobber, simPRO, ServiceTitan**) are all bolting AI onto huge installed bases. QuoteMate can no longer win on "we have AI."

3. **Only two competitors genuinely match the live-supplier-pricing claim — and both leave QuoteMate a gap.** **AroFlo** has the deepest (600+ connected suppliers, direct PO ordering, invoice auto-import) but is enterprise-priced with $900–2,900 onboarding. **Buildxact** has real auto-updating Bunnings/Mitre 10/Dahlsens catalogues + an AI assistant, but is builder-grade at $169–439/mo and skews to timber/hardware — **it has no visible Reece/Tradelink, so plumbers/gas-fitters are QuoteMate's cleanest win.**

4. **QuoteMate's flat $49/mo is simultaneously a strength and a liability.** Against per-user rivals (Tradify $48/user, Fergus $53+, Jobber +US$29/user) it's a bargain for any 3+ person crew, and the **free-forever plan is a genuine outlier** — almost nobody else has one. But against the sole-trader field it's the *priciest* option: Rounded $29.95, Thriday $29.95, MYOB Solo ~$11, QuotesYa $19, Zoho/Square/PayPal invoicing free. The premium is only justified by the quoting + materials + payments loop.

5. **The AU-payments + AU-tax seam is the structural moat against foreign entrants.** **Jobber has no native in-person payments in Australia. Housecall Pro is US/Canada-only with no Xero and no GST. ServiceTitan's AU launch is shallow and US-centric.** Square tap-to-pay that actually works in AU + auto-GST + Xero sync is a real wedge that US/Canadian giants can't easily copy.

**Biggest strategic watch-items:** ① the AI-clone price war (Insta Quotes & QuotesYa); ② **Zeller** — a A$1bn AU payments unicorn (1.4% cards, free banking, 110k merchants, explicitly targeting tradies, now building an AI-first POS) that could bolt on quoting; ③ **Thriday** (banking+books+invoicing+AI, now Tyro-backed) as the closest all-in-one AU concept; and ④ the **RBA surcharge ban from 1 Oct 2026**, which kills card-fee passthrough on Visa/MC/eftpos and makes QuoteMate's PayID/BPAY rails strategically more valuable.

## 5. Tier 1 — Direct AU/NZ tradie job-management (the core fight)

### Tradify
- **Positioning:** All-in-one job management for small trade businesses (1–20 staff). Broad workflow/admin tool, *not* quoting-first or AI-first.
- **Background:** Founded 2013, Auckland NZ (ex-electrician founder). **Acquired by The Access Group (UK), 1 Oct 2024.** ~20,000 customers (NZ/AU/UK), ~107 staff at acquisition.
- **Pricing (AUD/user/mo, monthly only — no annual, no free plan):** Lite **$48** · Pro **$52** · Plus **$62** (AI SmartRead/SmartWrite live only here). 14-day trial. *[verify: third-party sites show $36–40/user at higher seat counts — possible volume tiering.]* Pro × 5 users ≈ **$260/mo**.
- **Strengths:** Mature (12 yrs), deep scheduling/timesheets/compliance/POs, **Xero + MYOB + QuickBooks** all mature, full native Android, 4.7★ (~3,900 AU ratings), Access Group backing.
- **Weaknesses:** Per-user cost; no free tier; monthly-only; **no offline mode** (top complaint); clunky quoting front-end; shallow AI; **no live supplier pricing** (CSV import only).
- **QuoteMate wins:** Live supplier pricing; AI-native quoting; offline mode; Square tap-to-pay + PayID/BPAY vs Stripe-only; free plan + flat/annual pricing beats per-seat for any crew.
- **QuoteMate loses:** Breadth/maturity, deeper scheduling/timesheets, MYOB + QuickBooks, brand trust, thousands of reviews.

### ServiceM8
- **Positioning:** Cloud+mobile job management for small trade/home-service businesses. **The AU category leader / default incumbent.** Historically iOS-first.
- **Background:** Australian-made, HQ Darwin NT. Founded 2009/10, **bootstrapped/independent**, ~24 staff. Claims **50,000+ businesses** (AU/NZ + 40 countries).
- **Pricing (per-JOB, not per-user — its signature model):** Free (30 jobs, 1 user) · Starter **$29** (50 jobs) · Growing **$79** (150) · Premium **$149** (500) · Premium Plus **$349** (1,500). **All paid tiers = unlimited users.** Card fees 2.10%→1.49%. Add-ons inflate the bill (ServiceM8 Phone from $29/mo).
- **Strengths:** 15 yrs, huge install base, cheap unlimited-user crews, electronic **Forms/compliance** (solar/electrical), **real AI now** (AI quote/materials, AI email, and an **AI Phone Agent** that answers calls & books jobs), PayTo, strong two-way Xero/MYOB/QuickBooks.
- **Weaknesses:** **Android "Lite" is meaningfully worse than iOS** (its #1 complaint — and Android is the cheaper handset tradies actually buy); job-credit model surprises people; add-on cost creep; scales poorly past ~20 staff; **no live supplier pricing** (static import only).
- **QuoteMate wins:** True Android+iOS parity (attacks its biggest weakness); live supplier pricing vs static import; predictable pricing (no job-credit anxiety); PayID/BPAY breadth.
- **QuoteMate loses:** Maturity/trust, crew breadth (dispatch, GPS, route optimisation, timesheets→payroll, Form Store), **AI Phone Agent**, and **per-job pricing is genuinely cheaper for low-job/high-headcount crews.**
- **Realistic wedge:** Android-first tradies + live supplier pricing + simpler pricing — *not* out-featuring on breadth.

### Fergus
- **Positioning:** Cloud job-management for trade businesses (solo → ~60 staff); heritage in plumbing/electrical/HVAC. More an owner/office tool than an on-the-tools app.
- **Background:** Founded 2012, Auckland NZ (ex-plumber founder Dan Pollard). VC-backed (~A$16–20M raised). Markets "20,000–25,000 tradies."
- **Pricing:** Essentials from **$53/mo**, Pro **$75–77/mo**, Enterprise custom. Fundamentally **per-user**; monthly only, **no annual, no free plan.** *[verify: AU site may show NZD-converted figures.]* Reviews flag **frequent price rises and features re-locked into higher tiers post-signup.**
- **Strengths:** 13 yrs of job-costing/back-costing/profitability depth, POs, timesheets, SWMS; **100+ supplier integrations (Reece, Tradelink, Rexel, Mico) + Xero/MYOB/QuickBooks**; strong ANZ brand among plumbers/electricians.
- **Weaknesses:** Per-user cost + **price creep + feature re-locking** (its most consistent complaint); no annual/free plan; **"Fergus Go" mobile app widely criticised** (slow/failing loads); nascent bolt-on AI; supplier price-books are **static/account-negotiated, not live**, and not tied to AI quote generation.
- **QuoteMate wins:** AI voice/text → materials → *live* supplier pricing vs static price-books + manual assembly; free + flat/annual vs per-user + rising prices; mobile-first quality opening; Square + PayID/BPAY vs card-only 2.95%.
- **QuoteMate loses:** Job-costing/PO/timesheet depth, supplier+accounting breadth, installed base.

### AroFlo — *the honest live-pricing gap*
- **Positioning:** All-in-one for established, process-heavy trade/field operations. **Enterprise/mid-market**; sole traders steered to the lighter free "AroFloGo."
- **Background:** Founded 1998, Melbourne. **Acquired by Simpro Group (K1-backed) in Oct 2021.** Cites 30,000+ ANZ tradespeople.
- **Pricing (AUD, mostly quote-gated):** AroFloGo Self-Service **$0** · Lite ~**$40/mo** first user +$20 ea · Standard ~**$55/user** · Premium ~**$77/user (3-user min)**. **Setup/onboarding $899–$1,299 (complex from ~$2,900).** Real entry for a small commercial team = 4-figure setup + several hundred $/mo.
- **Strengths — the standout:** **Live, real-time supplier catalogues with your own negotiated pricing** — Reece, Rexel, Actrol, MM Electrical, Haymans, "600+ connected suppliers," **direct PO ordering to Reece/Rexel, auto-import of supplier invoices.** Deeper than anyone here. Plus SWMS/compliance (NECA partnership), projects/Gantt, inventory, new "Lightning" AI.
- **Weaknesses:** Cost & setup friction, steep learning curve ("designed by engineers"), dated UI (3.9★ AU iOS), weak offline.
- **QuoteMate wins:** Price & simplicity (free + $49 vs per-user + 3-user min + $900–2,900 onboarding), self-serve, sole-trader fit, modern AI-native UX.
- **⚠️ QuoteMate genuinely LOSES on supplier pricing here.** AroFlo's live, account-specific, two-way supplier integration + direct PO ordering is more mature than QuoteMate's. This is QuoteMate's clearest product gap — but AroFlo aims at a heavier buyer, so it's a "don't fight on their turf" situation, not an existential threat.

## 6. Tier 2 — Field-service & enterprise platforms

### simPRO
AU-founded (Brisbane, 2002), **>US$1B unicorn** (K1, 2021), now group-wide 24,000+ businesses / ~450k users (incl. AroFlo, ClockShark, BigChange). Built for **mid-size/commercial crews (~2–500 employees)** — overkill for a sole trader. **No public pricing** (~$300–600/mo for 5–10 users + often **$thousands–$10k+ implementation**, 3–5yr lock-in, ~8–12% annual rises). Deep estimating with real supplier catalogues/price books, AI estimating/scheduling, Xero + QuickBooks. **QuoteMate's play: "the anti-simPRO"** — transparent flat pricing, self-serve, instant value for the tradie who found simPRO too complex/expensive to onboard. They barely compete head-to-head.

### ServiceTitan (NASDAQ: TTAN)
US (Glendale, 2007), **IPO Dec 2024**, ~US$6–7B market cap, **FY26 revenue US$961M (+24%)**, ~9,500 customers. Built for **mid-market→enterprise (20+ techs with back-office)**. **No public pricing** (~US$250–500/tech/mo + **US$5–50k onboarding**, 12-mo minimum, **US$15–46k early-termination fees**). A 10-tech shop ≈ **US$55–86k Year 1**. Best-in-class dispatch/reporting/payments+financing; heavy AI ("Atlas"). **Launched in AU ~July 2024 but shallow/US-centric** — targets larger firms, localization gaps (dates, no native MYOB, limited Xero). **~20–100× QuoteMate's cost; they barely compete** — QuoteMate's adjacent play is AU tradies who priced ServiceTitan and recoiled.

### Jobber
Canada (Edmonton, 2011), ~US$237M raised, ~250k pros claimed (~100k paying *[verify]*). Built for **solo → small crews (1–15)** — the closest field-service peer to QuoteMate by target. **Pricing (USD, no AUD tier, no free plan):** Core **US$49/mo** (US$29 annual, 1 user) · Connect **US$139** · Grow **US$199** · Plus **US$699**; **+US$29/user**; add-ons stack (Marketing US$79, AI Receptionist US$99). Polished UX, strong scheduling/recurring/Client Hub, Jobber Copilot (AI coach, *not* materials takeoff). **Key AU gaps QuoteMate exploits:** **no native in-person payments in Australia**, **USD billing**, **manual GST**, Xero push-only, no supplier pricing. **QuoteMate wins on AU-fit; loses on brand maturity, dispatch depth, ecosystem.**

### Housecall Pro
US (San Diego, 2013), ~US$147M raised (the "US$5B valuation" premise is **unverified/likely false**), ~45,000 pros. **Pricing (USD, no free plan):** Basic **US$59** (1 user) · Essentials **US$149** (5) · MAX **US$299** (8, then +US$35/user). Strong payments (HCP Payments, ACH, **Wisetack financing**), mature mobile, AI CSR suite. **Effectively US/Canada-only** — no AU signup, **no GST, no Xero**, USD-only, US payment rails. **This is QuoteMate's single biggest structural advantage vs HCP: for an AU tradie it's largely unusable out of the box.**

### FieldPulse
US (Dallas, 2015), escalating funding (**$50M Series C, Aug 2025**), ~500 customers. SMB trades; ~US$99–500/mo per-seat, quoted, **no free trial, often 12-mo lock-in**. Genuinely sells in AU (localised site, **two-way Xero restricted to AU/NZ**) but AU is ~2% of base. **QuoteMate wins** on AU-native fit, transparent pricing + free tier, deeper AI; **loses** on breadth (dispatch/GPS/VoIP) and funding.

### Ascora
AU (Perth), end-to-end job management, solo → 30–50 techs. **Pricing (AUD, published):** Essentials **$45/user** · Elite **$59/user** (most popular) · Enterprise custom; setup fee on Elite/Enterprise. **Strongest AU accounting stack: Xero + MYOB + QuickBooks** (QuoteMate is Xero-only), plus **Reece price-file sync** (single-supplier, monthly), Stripe Tap to Pay. Complaints: timesheet reliability, QuickBooks sync, onboarding curve. **QuoteMate wins** on price simplicity/no onboarding, solo-friendliness, multi-supplier live/AI pricing + voice-to-quote; **loses** if the tradie needs MYOB or multi-crew dispatch/equipment/project management.

### GeoOp / GeoNext — *being wound down*
NZ (Auckland, 2009), 10k+ users. **Acquired by The Access Group Feb 2025 — no longer accepting sign-ups; customers being force-migrated to Tradify.** Buggy legacy app, no offline, painful forced migration (manual re-entry). **Opportunity:** actively target **frustrated GeoOp/GeoNext refugees** who don't want to be pushed onto Tradify — a live, disrupted install base right now.

### Formitize
AU (Sydney, 2012), forms/compliance-heavy trades (pest control, inspections, safety). **A$29.99/user/mo.** Smart forms, SWMS, quoting/invoicing, Xero/QuickBooks. Steep form-builder learning curve. **QuoteMate wins** on speed/simplicity for the quote→pay loop + generative AI + live pricing; **loses** for compliance/inspection-heavy businesses needing deep custom forms.

### TradiePad / "Tradie Toolbox" — *not a product competitor*
**TradiePad is an AU consultancy** (setup/training/migration across 30+ vendors — ServiceM8, simPRO, AroFlo, Tradify, etc.), not SaaS. Best treated as a **potential partnership/reseller channel** (note: they currently steer ex-GeoOp users to Tradify — QuoteMate would be displacing that recommendation). "Tradie Toolbox" = a QBCC government education initiative + a generic listicle metaphor; no product overlap.

## 7. Tier 3 — Quoting / estimating specialists

### Buildxact — *the key incumbent threat to the live-pricing claim*
AU-born (Melbourne, 2011), **A$18.5M Series A (2022)**, claims 25,000+ builders *[verify]*. Construction estimating + job management for **residential builders/renovators** (bigger jobs than a sole tradie). **Pricing (AUD):** Entry **$169/mo** (or $133 annual) · Pro **$279** · Teams **$439**; +$67–87/user. **Genuinely matches QuoteMate's headline:** live, auto-updating **Bunnings / Mitre 10 / Dahlsens** catalogues + **"Blu" AI** (takeoff/estimate generator). **But:** desktop-heavy, needs plan takeoff, real learning curve, and **no visible Reece/Tradelink** (skews timber/hardware). **QuoteMate wins** on price ($49 vs $169+), mobile-first speed, and **plumbing suppliers (Reece/Tradelink)**; **loses** on estimating depth for whole-house builds. *Positioning line: "Buildxact is for building companies estimating whole houses; QuoteMate is for the tradie who needs a professional quote before he leaves the driveway."*

### Quotient
Global CPQ/online quoting (NZ heritage), **not tradie- or AU-specific.** US$28–48/mo. Great quote polish + tracking + e-sign, but **no live supplier pricing, no AI, no invoicing/payments/jobs** (quote-only). QuoteMate wins decisively for AU tradies (mobile, AI, live pricing, payments, GST); Quotient wins on desktop quote maturity across industries.

### Databuild
AU/NZ (est. 1975, ITW-owned), **Windows desktop** building-business management (estimating/accounting/payroll) for **building companies**, not sole tradies. No public pricing, no free trial, no live-API pricing/AI/mobile. Legacy — not a direct sole-trader competitor.

### PandaDoc / Proposify / (Quote Roller — discontinued)
Global proposal/CPQ + e-signature for **sales teams, cross-industry**. PandaDoc is a US$1B unicorn (US$100M ARR). US$19–65/user/mo. Beautiful proposals + e-sign, but **no supplier pricing, no trade context, no AU tax, per-user cost.** QuoteMate wins decisively for tradies; these serve office/B2B sales.

### Groundplan
AU (2013), **on-screen takeoff + estimating** for plan-based trades (electrical/plumbing/HVAC/landscaping). ~$69–75/mo *[verify currency]* + "Count Assist" AI counting. Integrates with **Simpro/Fergus** for the job side — *not* a full quote-to-cash tool. QuoteMate wins on all-in-one + instant plain-English quoting; Groundplan wins on takeoff precision for plan-heavy jobs.

### Countfire / Cubit / Bluebeam
Professional **takeoff/estimator** tools for larger commercial/residential jobs & drawings. Countfire (UK electrical takeoff); **Cubit/Buildsoft** (AU, from $167+GST/user/mo + AI add-on); Bluebeam Revu (PDF markup/measurement, ~US$260–350/yr). A different, more technical buyer. None threaten the sole-tradie fast-quote niche directly.

## 8. Tier 4 — Emerging AI-native quoting apps (highest-velocity threat)

> These copy QuoteMate's *exact* positioning and are the top priority to monitor. The core pitch — voice/text/photo → 30-second priced quote — is now **table stakes, not a differentiator.**

### Insta Quotes (instaquotes.co.nz) — *most feature-complete rival*
AU+NZ, "Mate AI." Quote in <30s from **voice, text, photo, or engineering drawings**; 42 trades; AU & NZ rates. **Pricing (AUD): $29 (30 quotes) / $59 (60) / $99 (unlimited)**, 7-day trial. **Already ships:** branded PDF + Word export, **online acceptance + e-signature, Stripe deposit collection, auto-generated tax invoices, Xero + ServiceM8 integrations.** It matches most of QuoteMate's quote→accept→invoice→deposit loop at similar/lower price. **QuoteMate's edge:** native **in-person Square tap-to-pay + PayID/BPAY** (Insta = Stripe *online deposits only*), plus verifiable *named live* supplier feeds vs "current market rates."

### QuotesYa (quotesya.com) — *cheapest AU clone*
AU-focused, "quote in 30 seconds," voice input, claims **"real Australian supplier prices"** (suppliers **unnamed** *[verify]*). **Free (3 quotes) → Pro $19/mo (promo) → $35/mo.** Undercuts QuoteMate on price and attacks the live-pricing claim head-on — but discloses it doesn't guarantee accuracy (a wedge if QuoteMate's pricing is genuinely verifiable), and in-app payments unclear.

### QuoteIQ (US), Handoff / 1build (US), Bild AI (US)
- **QuoteIQ** — US, 40+ trades, photo/voice → estimate, US$29.99–699.99. US-centric (no AU feeds) but proves the model scales to 40+ trades.
- **Handoff / 1build** — **best-funded AI-native estimator** (~US$25M raised; Nemetschek, YC, Greycroft). Estimates from PDF/voice/text on 100k+ projects & 60M+ SKUs; **the 1build real-time cost API is the direct analogue-and-threat to QuoteMate's live-pricing moat.** US residential focus today — but the clearest template a well-funded AU localiser could copy.
- **Bild AI** (YC W25, Khosla) — blueprint→bid for commercial door/hardware; not a tradie competitor, but evidence VC is funding AI estimating across every construction vertical.

### Incumbents adding AI (the quieter, more dangerous vector)
**ServiceM8, Tradify, simPRO, AroFlo, Fergus, Jobber** are all bolting AI onto huge installed bases. They don't need to win on AI — just "good-enough AI + everything you already use here." Also note **Voxworks** (AI phone agent → job cards in ServiceM8/Simpro/AroFlo/Fergus/Jobber) and **Flowtivity** (bespoke AU "AI quoting engines" from ~$5k) educating the market.

## 9. Tier 5 — Invoicing / accounting / contractor-finance

> **None of these has live AU supplier pricing, AI materials takeoff, or a trade job pipeline.** But the accounting incumbents own the ledger + accountant relationship — QuoteMate's structural disadvantage, which it neutralises by *integrating* (Xero sync) rather than replacing the books.

- **Invoice2go (from BILL):** AU-born (2002), trade-heavy (~24% of reviewers in construction). $10.83–~$45+/mo (invoice caps on lower tiers). **Ugly review sentiment QuoteMate can contrast against:** renewal price hikes, "subscription/cancel trap," **funds held / not paid out**, account deactivations. QuoteMate wins on live pricing + pipeline + tap-to-pay + free tier + "funds not held hostage."
- **Xero — partner *and* competitor:** AU leader (~**1.77M AU subscribers**, ~55–60% advisor share). Does quotes/invoices natively. Ignite ~$35 (20-invoice cap) → Ultimate ~$130+. **Xero Go sole-trader app retired Sept 2024** — no cheap mobile tier now. **Position QuoteMate as "the front-of-van tool that *feeds* Xero," not a replacement.**
- **MYOB:** #2 (~17–25% advisor share, KKR-owned). **Solo ~$11/mo but can't even do quotes**; Business Lite/Pro/AccountRight $315–$165/mo. Brutal billing/cancellation/support complaints — an attack surface. QuoteMate is quoting-first where Solo isn't.
- **QuickBooks (Intuit):** #3 (~8%). Simple Start ~$33 has **unlimited quotes+invoices** (beats Xero Ignite's cap & MYOB Solo's no-quotes); Plus adds project/job tracking. Aggressive promos undercut on pure invoicing. Concede "fine for the books," win the on-site quoting layer.
- **Rounded (AU, 2015):** Sole-trader invoicing/BAS. **$23.95–$29.95/mo — cheaper than QuoteMate Pro.** Slick, AU-native, but solo-only, no supplier pricing/pipeline. QuoteMate must justify the ~$19 premium on quoting/materials value.
- **Thriday (AU) — closest all-in-one concept:** Banking + invoicing + accounting + tax + AI, tradie-targeted. Free / $29.95 / $119. **Being acquired by Tyro (Dec 2025)** — banking+payments+books converging. Bank-account-as-ledger is sticky. **Watch closely.**
- **Hnry (AU/NZ):** Pay-as-you-go tax service — **1% + GST of income (capped $1,500/yr)**, no subscription; auto-pays tax/GST/BAS. Different job; can **coexist** with QuoteMate (quote in QuoteMate, tax via Hnry).
- **Zoho Invoice (free) / Books:** Cheap, feature-rich, not AU-trade-specific. Concede the price war, win the workflow.
- **Square Invoices / PayPal Invoicing:** Free/cheap, payment-first, workflow-thin. Square is QuoteMate's *own rails* — a "frenemy"; differentiate on everything upstream of the payment. **Zettle (PayPal) tap-to-pay is not available in AU** — a gap QuoteMate+Square fills.

## 10. Tier 6 — Payments-layer competitors

- **Square (Block):** QuoteMate's rails *and* a substitute. In-person **1.6%** (post-May-2024 sellers), online 2.2%, no monthly fee, free Square Invoices + Tap to Pay. A tradie who just wants to get paid can use Square directly and skip QuoteMate. QuoteMate's stacked fee (1% Pro / 1.7% Free) sits *on top* of Square's ~1.6%, so it looks pricier on raw rate — **justify with the integrated quote→paid loop, not price.**
- **Zeller — the AU unicorn to watch:** **~A$96M ARR, 110,000+ AU customers, A$1bn+ valuation** (ex-Square AU boss founder). **1.4% in-person cards** (cheaper than QuoteMate's stacked cost), **1.7%+25c invoicing, free business account, no monthly fee.** Explicitly markets "EFTPOS & Invoicing for Tradespeople" and is **launching an AI-first cloud POS.** If Zeller bolts credible AI quoting onto invoicing, it becomes a near-frontal competitor with a payments cost advantage + 110k merchants to upsell. **Highest structural payments threat.**
- **Stripe:** Developer-first; ~1.75%+30c+GST; **Tap to Pay in-person ~2.7%** (pricier in-person than Square/Zeller/Tyro). Low direct-tradie threat, but it's the rail some AI clones (Insta Quotes) use, and QuoteMate's *own* subscription billing runs on it.
- **Tyro (ASX):** ~1.4% MSF, terminal-oriented, health/hospitality/retail heavy. Competes for payments volume but has no quoting/AI story. Now acquiring Thriday (banking+books+payments convergence).

**⚠️ Regulatory watch — RBA surcharge ban (from 1 Oct 2026):** Surcharging on **eftpos/Visa/Mastercard (debit + credit) will be banned nationally**, with interchange caps cut. **Impact on QuoteMate:** the optional "2.9% surcharge passthrough" on card rails becomes **illegal for those networks** — the tradie must absorb the fee, sharpening price sensitivity vs Square/Zeller's lower base rates. **But the ban does NOT cover PayID, BPAY, bank transfer, PayPal or Amex** — so QuoteMate's **PayID/BPAY rails become strategically more valuable** (not surcharge-banned, near-zero cost). **Lean into "pay by PayID — no card fees" messaging before Oct 2026.** *[verify exact platform-fee-passthrough treatment with counsel.]*

## 11. Tier 7 — DIY substitutes (the willingness-to-pay ceiling)

- **ChatGPT / Gemini / Copilot / Claude (~A$30–35/mo):** Tradies paste a job description and get a plausible line-item quote + customer email in seconds. Marketing blogs actively coach this; there's a "Construction Estimation GPT." **Falls short:** hallucinated/US/stale prices (no live AU supplier data), no branded PDF, **no payment**, no GST-compliance object, inconsistent output. **Real threat = a *floor*, not a replacement:** a $0–35/mo AI + a saved template + Square is "good enough" for a solo doing 3 quotes/week, capping willingness to pay $49/mo (consistent with QuoteMate's ~1.5% trial→paid). **The moat is the integrated loop (live prices + PDF + payment + records), not the AI** — because AI quote-writing is commoditising fast. *Watch: custom GPTs with uploaded rate cards narrow the "AI can't quote accurately" claim.*
- **Spreadsheets / free templates (Excel, Sheets, Numbers; QuickBooks/Square/Inv24 free templates):** The incumbent behaviour. Free, total control, offline, zero learning curve. **Switching friction = the real enemy** (sunk familiarity, "it's free," no acute pain until they scale or chase unpaid invoices). QuoteMate wins on speed + branded output + live pricing + **getting paid on the spot** (a spreadsheet can never take a tap-to-pay deposit). *Per your standing instruction: do **not** counter these with a free QuoteMate quote/invoice generator — it would cannibalise the paid app.*

## 14. Strategic synthesis — moats, gaps, threats, opportunities

**Durable moats (defend & amplify):**
1. **Live, named AU supplier pricing** (Bunnings/Reece/Tradelink) — verifiable, hard to replicate, unmatched at this price point. *The* headline.
2. **AU in-person payments** (Square tap-to-pay + PayID/BPAY) — foreign giants can't easily match; AI clones only do online deposits.
3. **AU-native tax/compliance** (auto-GST/ABN) + **Xero sync** — kills US/Canadian entrants for AU buyers.
4. **Flat pricing + free-forever tier** — a genuine structural outlier in a per-user, quote-gated, add-on-creep market.
5. **Android/iOS parity + offline** — directly attacks ServiceM8 (weak Android) and Fergus (buggy app).

**Honest gaps (where QuoteMate loses today):**
1. **MYOB & QuickBooks integration** — Xero-only; Ascora/simPRO/Tradify/GeoOp cover MYOB. A real hole for MYOB-committed tradies.
2. **Supplier-pricing depth** — AroFlo's two-way + direct PO ordering is more mature.
3. **Breadth at scale** — no multi-crew dispatch, GPS/routing, payroll, job costing, inventory, marketing/reviews suite. Customers outgrow QuoteMate toward simPRO/ServiceTitan/Ascora as they scale.
4. **Price vs solo invoicing tools** and **vs AI clones** ($19–29).
5. **Brand/trust** — incumbents have 12–15 yrs and thousands of reviews.

**Rising threats (monitor):**
1. **AI-clone price war** — Insta Quotes (most complete) & QuotesYa (cheapest) at half QuoteMate's price.
2. **Zeller** — payments-cost advantage + 110k merchants + AI POS ambition.
3. **Thriday/Tyro** — converging banking+books+payments all-in-one.
4. **Incumbents' AI** — ServiceM8/Tradify/Jobber adding "good-enough AI" to sticky bases.
5. **RBA surcharge ban (Oct 2026)** — pressures the card-fee-passthrough model.

**Best target segments to attack:**
- AU sole traders/small crews who trialled **simPRO/ServiceTitan/AroFlo** and found them too complex/expensive/slow to onboard.
- **GeoOp/GeoNext refugees** being force-migrated to Tradify right now (live, disrupted base).
- AU tradies eyeing **Jobber/Housecall Pro** who hit the **no-AU-payments / no-GST / USD-billing** wall.
- **Android-first tradies** underserved by ServiceM8.
- **Plumbers/gas-fitters** (Reece/Tradelink) where Buildxact's timber/hardware catalogues don't reach.

## 15. Positioning playbook (recommended messaging)

1. **Lead with live supplier pricing, not "AI."** "AI quoting" is now table stakes. "Quote with **today's real Bunnings/Reece/Tradelink prices**" is not. Publicise the *named* feeds — it's the wedge against QuotesYa/Insta Quotes ("their prices are guessed; ours are real").
2. **Own "quote *and* get paid on the doorstep."** Tap-to-pay + PayID/BPAY is the differentiator vs both the AI clones (online-deposit-only) and the accounting tools (no on-site payment).
3. **Frame vs the giants as "the anti-simPRO / anti-ServiceTitan"** — transparent flat pricing, no onboarding project, no lock-in, built for the tradie they over-serve.
4. **Frame vs Xero/MYOB/QuickBooks as "feeds your accountant, doesn't replace them."** Stay the partner.
5. **Attack the ugly review surfaces:** Invoice2go's held funds / cancel trap; MYOB's billing/support pain; per-user + add-on creep everywhere; Jobber/HCP's "no AU payments / no GST."
6. **Pre-empt the surcharge ban:** "Pay by PayID — no card fees" becomes a genuine selling point from Oct 2026.
7. **Close the MYOB gap** on the roadmap — it's the most-cited integration hole vs Ascora/Tradify/simPRO.
