# FB news-style headline research — 2026-07-26

Inputs for the v2 headline set in `marketing/facebook-ads-playbook-2026-07.md`. Four streams: Sabri Suby's verbatim ads (tutorial transcript + Meta Ad Library trackers), classic/native advertorial headline science, Aussie tradie language + citable stats, and a live AU Meta Ad Library sweep of the tradie-software ecosystem.

## 1. How Suby actually writes breaking-news headlines

- **Third-person gossip-press voice about a person/identity group** — news *about* someone, never first person: "Shark Tank investor drops client-getting bombshell", "Shark Tank investor breaks his silence". The credibility/identity marker does the audience targeting.
- **Swipe-and-deploy from gossip headlines**: Taylor Swift fan headline → "Aussies going wild after spotting home loan hack". Template: [audience identity] + [gossip/drama verb] + [niche subject].
- **Ranged odd numbers**: "90 to 480 qualified leads per month", "12 to 19 kilos in 5 weeks". Specificity adds believability; round numbers read as invented.
- **Intrigue WITH benefit** — blind clickbait ("You won't believe what Ivanka does in the morning") fails because the algorithm can't find the audience; benefit-only fails because it's boring. The intersection wins.
- **Avoid "you/your" in ads** (Meta personal call-out penalty); body copy first-person, headlines third-person.
- Anatomy: pattern-interrupt image → headline → slippery lead-in (changing only the lead-in has 5x'd ROAS) → intriguing link description → line-by-line "butter" body copy → **Learn More** only.
- His words: "If I had to bet my life on something working, it would always be the breaking news."

## 2. Headline science (measured)

- **Negativity lifts CTR**: Outbrain 65k-title study — negative superlatives +63% vs positive; Nature 2023 (105k Upworthy A/B tests) — each negative word ≈ +2.3% clicks. Suby's counter-claim (positive beats pain 8/10) means: **test both frames, let data decide**.
- **Question headlines** (self-diagnosis, "Do you make these mistakes in English?") can ~double CTR; ran unbeaten 40 years in direct mail.
- **Story-not-claim**: report something that happened ("They laughed when I sat down at the piano"), don't make a promise.
- **Parenthetical curiosity tags** "(and it's not what you think)" — bracketed clarifications measured +38% CTR.
- 80–95 characters is the CTR sweet spot; specific odd numbers beat round ones.
- Compliance line: borrow the news *layout*, never fabricate stories/people presented as real journalism (FTC has acted on fake-news advertorials). Fictional masthead, true claims, real stats only.

## 3. Tradie language (verbatim, from r/AusElectricians, r/tradies, r/AusRenovation, Whirlpool)

Best raw material:
- "That quote you spent 4 hours on was lost to someone else who just pulled a number out of the hat"
- "A 20-minute job comes with 2 hours of paperwork"
- Free quotes are "the biggest unpaid labour racket in the country" (r/tradies, 190+ comments)
- "Call three tradies, one calls back" (430-upvote thread)
- "You finish a day's work, then come home and catch up on all the invoicing you promised you would do"
- Wearing every hat: "electrician, salesman, accountant, bookkeeper, safety officer, debt collector"
- Self-deprecation register: "can't do paperwork for shit", "don't be a potato with the admin"

Vocabulary rules: **quote** (never "estimate"), **tradie/sparky/chippy** (never "contractor"), *paperwork, admin, the books, chasing money, on/off the tools, tyre kickers, ghosting*. Never SaaS-speak (streamline/workflow/solution/empower/game-changer). No overclaims ("never chase a payment again" reads false — the credible promise is speed). Skip the "missus does the books" trope (real but gendered; conflicts with tone rule).

## 4. Citable statistics (verified primary sources ONLY)

- **Hnry Sole Trader Pulse**: tradies spend **~8 hours a week** on financial admin.
- **Xero Small Business Insights**: **48% of AU small-business invoices paid late**; average **22.6 days** to get paid; late payments cost small businesses **$15,257/yr** on average, **$1.1B/yr** nationally.
- ServiceSeeking: 52% of customers get quotes within 1 hour on-platform.
- Vendor-attributed only: Tradify "save ~10 hours/week", ServiceM8 "30% more work in first 3 months".
- **Do NOT cite** (SEO-farm numbers with no primary source): "70% of leads go to first responder", "90% of contracts to first reply", "tradies lose 15+ hours/week (Xero/ABS)".

## 5. Live AU Ad Library patterns (Tradify/ServiceM8 ecosystem, Jul 2026)

- Named-person hyper-specific story ads run now: "Pat had 363 pricing bundles in ServiceM8. Double-ups. Wrong codes."
- Headlines: "Get Off The Tools At 5pm", "Stop Losing Weekends to Quoting", "Don't go out on your own before you read this", "Stop guessing your prices" ("Built by a sparky, for tradies").
- The "second shift" frame (quoting/invoicing after a 10-hour day) is the dominant pain angle across every advertiser.
- Trial reassurance is always concrete: "No credit card, no catch."
