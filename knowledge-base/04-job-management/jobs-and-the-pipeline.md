# Managing jobs and the pipeline

QuoteMate isn't just quoting — it tracks each job from first enquiry through to paid, so you can see where everything stands from the dashboard.

## The 9-stage pipeline

Every job moves through these stages, each stamped with a time:

**Inquiry → Quoted → Accepted → Scheduled → In progress → Completed → Paid → Closed** (plus **Cancelled**).

Update a job's stage as work progresses and see your whole pipeline at a glance on the dashboard.

## Scheduling

Schedule jobs with a date so you know what's on. You can connect **Google Calendar** in Settings to keep scheduled jobs alongside your calendar. *(See the note below on calendar sync.)*

> **Calendar sync note:** connecting Google Calendar is available in Settings. If you rely on scheduled jobs appearing automatically on your Google Calendar, confirm the behaviour on your device first — see [Google Calendar integration](../05-integrations/google-calendar-integration.md).

## On-site photos

Attach photos to a job and **annotate them** with arrows, circles, and dimensions — handy for recording site conditions, marking up where things go, or documenting completed work.

## Checklists

Add **checklists** to a job for materials and to-dos, so nothing gets missed on-site.

## Follow-ups

QuoteMate can automatically follow up on quotes that haven't been accepted and remind customers about unpaid invoices. See [Tracking payments and reminders](../03-invoicing-and-payments/tracking-payments-and-reminders.md).
