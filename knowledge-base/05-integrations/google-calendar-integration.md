# Google Calendar integration

QuoteMate can connect to Google Calendar so your scheduled jobs sit alongside the rest of your week.

## Connecting

Go to **Settings → Google Calendar** and connect with your Google account.

## What to expect

Connecting authorises QuoteMate to work with your Google Calendar. If having scheduled jobs appear on your Google Calendar automatically is important to your workflow, check that it's behaving the way you expect on your device before relying on it, and reach out to support if anything looks off. Scheduling within QuoteMate always keeps your jobs organised inside the app regardless.
