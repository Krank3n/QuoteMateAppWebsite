# Reece maX integration

QuoteMate has a live **Reece maX** integration — the only Australian quoting app that does. For plumbers and gasfitters, it means every line in a plumbing quote prices at *your* Reece trade rate, not RRP.

## Connecting Reece maX

Go to **Settings → Connect Reece** and connect with OAuth (about a minute). You'll need an existing Reece maX account — if you don't have one, sign up at reece.com.au/max. On first connect, QuoteMate pulls your full purchasable catalogue at your trade-discounted rates, then refreshes it nightly. You can trigger a manual refresh under **Settings → Integrations → Reece**.

Reece is **on by default for plumbers and gasfitters**. Other trades can connect too and still get trade-rate pricing on matched items.

## Ordering from Reece

Once a customer accepts a quote, you can **order the materials to Reece from inside the job**: pick your branch, choose pickup or delivery, set a required-by date, and the order lands on your Reece account.

## Cost

The Reece integration is **included at no extra cost** on every paid plan and during the trial. There's no per-order surcharge.
