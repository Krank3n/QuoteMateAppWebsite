# Frequently asked questions

Short answers to the questions customers ask most. Each links to the full article.

## Getting started

**What is QuoteMate?**
An AI-powered quoting and invoicing app for Australian tradies. Describe a job, get a priced materials list, send a branded quote, and get paid. → [What is QuoteMate?](01-getting-started/what-is-quotemate.md)

**What trades does it support?**
All trades — 24+ including carpentry, plumbing, electrical, building, and cleaning. Templates cover common jobs; you can describe any custom job in plain English. → [Trades supported](01-getting-started/trades-supported.md)

**What devices does it run on?**
iPhone, iPad, Android, and web. One subscription covers all of them and your data syncs. → [Supported platforms](01-getting-started/supported-platforms-and-devices.md)

**Can I use it offline?**
Yes. Create and edit quotes with no signal; everything syncs when you're back online. → [Supported platforms](01-getting-started/supported-platforms-and-devices.md)

## Quoting

**How does the AI quoting work?**
Describe the job in plain English — like *"Build a 4x3 metre hardwood deck with stairs"* — and QuoteMate suggests the materials and quantities with live pricing. You edit anything. → [AI material suggestions](02-quoting/ai-material-suggestions.md)

**Where does the pricing come from?**
Live prices from major Australian suppliers, but your own saved trade rates and supplier favourites come first. You can import a supplier price list from a photo or PDF. → [Live supplier pricing](02-quoting/supplier-pricing.md)

**Does it calculate GST?**
Yes — 10% GST is calculated and shown automatically on every quote and invoice. → [GST, labour, markup, and travel](02-quoting/gst-labour-markup-and-travel.md)

## Pricing and billing

**Is there a free plan?**
Yes — Free forever, no credit card, with **unlimited quotes and invoices**. Customers pay online via Square; QuoteMate's 1.7% platform fee comes out of **your payout**, not the customer's bill. → [Plans and pricing](06-pricing-and-billing/plans-and-pricing.md)

**How much is Pro?**
$49/month or $328/year (about $27.33/month). → [Plans and pricing](06-pricing-and-billing/plans-and-pricing.md)

**How does the free trial work?**
A 14-day Pro trial that starts when you create your first quote. No card needed. → [Free trial](06-pricing-and-billing/free-trial.md)

**Can I cancel anytime?**
Yes. Cancel from the app store (or the Stripe portal on web). You keep Pro until the end of your billing period, then move to Free. → [Managing your subscription](06-pricing-and-billing/managing-your-subscription.md)

## Getting paid

**How do I take payments?**
Square Tap to Pay on-site (tap a card or phone on your device), or an online payment link on the invoice. Also bank transfer, PayID, BPAY, PayPal, card, cash, and cheque. → [Getting paid with Square](03-invoicing-and-payments/getting-paid-with-square.md)

**What are the fees?**
Square's processing fee plus a QuoteMate platform fee. On Pro that's 1.0% online / 1.5% in-person (vs 1.7% on Free). You can optionally pass a 2.9% surcharge to the customer. → [Getting paid with Square](03-invoicing-and-payments/getting-paid-with-square.md)

**How do I refund a customer?**
Through your Square account — there's no refund button in the app. → [Troubleshooting](08-troubleshooting/troubleshooting-common-issues.md)

## Jobs and integrations

**Can I manage jobs after the quote is sent?**
Yes — a 9-stage pipeline with scheduling, on-site photos with annotation, checklists, and automated follow-ups. → [Jobs and the pipeline](04-job-management/jobs-and-the-pipeline.md)

**Does it integrate with Xero?**
Yes — push invoices in one tap, auto-create contacts, bulk sync, and export Xero-compatible CSVs. → [Xero integration](05-integrations/xero-integration.md)

**Does it work with Reece?**
Yes — a live Reece maX integration prices plumbing quotes at your trade rate, and you can order to Reece from inside an accepted quote. → [Reece maX integration](05-integrations/reece-max-integration.md)

## Account and support

**Is my data secure?**
Yes — stored on Google Firebase with encryption in transit and at rest, compliant with the Australian Privacy Act. QuoteMate doesn't sell your data. → [Data security and privacy](07-account-and-settings/data-security-and-privacy.md)

**How do I contact support?**
Email tom@hansendev.com.au or use the website contact form. Pro gets priority support. → [Troubleshooting](08-troubleshooting/troubleshooting-common-issues.md)
