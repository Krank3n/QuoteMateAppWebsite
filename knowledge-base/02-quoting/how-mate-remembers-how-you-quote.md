# How Mate remembers the way you quote

Tell Mate something once about how you price, and it can follow it on every quote after that. Your rates, your rules, and the way you work are kept in one place you can read and edit.

## Telling Mate in chat

Say it the way you'd say it to an apprentice: "Add a $90 call-out fee to every job", "I always allow 10% extra on materials for waste", "End of lease clean is $380 a job including materials."

Mate replies with a card. A **Remember how you quote** card holds a rule; a **Save a rate** card holds a price for a type of work. Tap **Remember it** or **Save rate** to keep it, or **Dismiss** if it misread you. Nothing is saved until you tap.

On your very first job, while nothing is saved yet, Mate asks one question about how you quote: whether you set a rate for the job or work it up from materials and labour. That answer is saved the same way.

## Seeing what's saved

Go to **Settings → Trade & Pricing** and scroll to the **How you quote** card. It lists every rule and every rate on your rate card, with the rate's basis (per job, per hour, per square metre and so on), whether it includes GST, and whether materials are included.

## Changing it

- Tap the **⊗** beside a rule or a rate to remove it. It's gone straight away and won't apply to the next quote.
- Tap **Add a rule** to type a rule by hand, one plain sentence.
- Tap **Add a rate** to add a price for a type of work, with the unit, GST basis and whether materials are included.

Changes here save immediately, outside the screen's Save button, because a wrong rule left in place would be applied to the very next quote.

## What it's used for

Every quote Mate drafts follows the saved rules, and it reaches for a saved rate whenever the job matches one. Supplier preferences and the hardware stores you've chosen live on the same screen and work the same way: Mate prices from your suppliers first.
