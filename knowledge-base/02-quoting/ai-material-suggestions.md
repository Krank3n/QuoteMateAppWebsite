# How AI material suggestions work

Describe a job in plain English and QuoteMate's AI suggests the materials and quantities you need, then applies live supplier pricing. It's a starting point that saves you building the list from scratch — you stay in control and edit anything.

## How to use it

Type or dictate a description of the job, for example:

> *"Build a 4x3 metre hardwood deck with stairs"*

QuoteMate returns a materials list with estimated quantities, suggested labour hours, and the items grouped into sensible work sections. You then review, adjust quantities, swap items, and fetch prices.

## What the AI does and doesn't do

- **It suggests** — materials, quantities, and labour hours based on your description.
- **You decide** — every line is editable. Change anything that doesn't match how you do the job.
- Treat AI quantities as a fast first draft, not gospel. Check them against the real job before you send.

AI material and title generation is a **Pro feature** (included in your 14-day trial). On the Free plan you can still build quotes manually with live pricing.

## About the AI

AI suggestions are generated by QuoteMate's AI service. Job descriptions may be processed by third-party AI services to produce suggestions; no personal or banking details are sent. See [Data security and privacy](../07-account-and-settings/data-security-and-privacy.md).
