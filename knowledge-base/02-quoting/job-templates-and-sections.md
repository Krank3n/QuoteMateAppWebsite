# Job templates and section templates

Templates are how you stop rebuilding the same quote over and over. QuoteMate has two kinds: built-in job templates to start a quote fast, and your own saved section templates for the parts you quote again and again.

## Built-in job templates

QuoteMate ships with templates for common jobs — decks, fences, pergolas, stairs, and more. Each template has parameters (like deck area, fence length, or number of steps) that drive the quantities, so you enter a few numbers and get a priced starting list.

## Section templates (your own)

A section is a named chunk of a job with its own materials, labour, and margin. Once you've priced a section you do often — a metre of Colorbond fence, a gate hang, a downlight rough-in — save it as a **section template** and drop it into any future quote in one tap.

This keeps your pricing consistent and your quotes fast, especially for repeat work.
