# Live supplier pricing and your trade rates

QuoteMate prices materials from real Australian suppliers, but it always puts *your* numbers first. If you've saved a trade rate or a supplier price for an item, that's what it uses — retail pricing is only a fallback.

## Where prices come from

QuoteMate looks for each material's price in this order, so your own data always wins:

1. **Your saved section templates** — bundles you've priced before.
2. **Your supplier favourites** — your personal price book at your real trade rates.
3. **Your configured suppliers** — live prices pulled from the hardware and trade suppliers you've set up in the app.
4. **AI estimate** — a fallback when nothing else returns a price.

Plumbers connected to **Reece maX** get live Reece trade-rate pricing on matched items. See [Reece maX integration](../05-integrations/reece-max-integration.md).

## Import your own price list

Snap a photo or upload a PDF of a supplier price list and QuoteMate extracts each line item. Review the rows, then save the ones you want to your favourites so they price automatically next time.

## Save your trade rates

Save what you actually pay (and charge) for common items. QuoteMate remembers what you charged last time to keep your pricing consistent across quotes.

## "No prices found" — what to do

If an item comes back without a price, try one of these:

- **Reword the search** — match the supplier's shelf name (products don't always match how you'd say it).
- **Save a supplier favourite** for that item so it prices instantly next time.
- **Fetch prices again** after editing the item name.
