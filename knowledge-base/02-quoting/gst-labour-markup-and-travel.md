# GST, labour, markup, and travel

QuoteMate handles the Australian pricing bits for you — GST, labour, markup, and travel — so your quote adds up correctly and stays compliant.

## GST

QuoteMate automatically calculates and displays **10% GST** on all quotes and invoices. You can set whether your stored supplier prices are entered ex-GST (the default) or inc-GST; either way the GST component is worked out and shown on the document.

## Labour

Add labour as **hours or days**, with your labour rate. If your quote is split into sections, you can set labour per section. There's a sensible default labour rate you set during onboarding, and you can change it any time in Settings.

## Markup

You can apply **separate markup to materials and to labour**, so you're not forced into one blended margin. Set defaults in Settings and override per quote.

## Travel

Add a **travel adjustment** for jobs away from your base. QuoteMate includes a fuel-cost estimator based on the job address to help you put a fair travel figure on the quote.
