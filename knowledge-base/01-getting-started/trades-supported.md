# Trades QuoteMate supports

QuoteMate is built for all trades. The smart templates and pricing cover common jobs like decks, fences, pergolas, bathroom renos, and more, and you can describe any custom job in plain English if there's no template for it.

## Supported trades (24+)

Electricians, Plumbers, Carpenters, Painters, Landscapers, Builders, Roofers, Tilers, Concreters, Fencers, Cleaners, HVAC technicians, Renderers, Glaziers, Solar installers, Cabinet makers, Plasterers, Flooring installers, Locksmiths, Pest controllers, Demolition contractors, Garage door installers, Welders & fabricators, and Arborists.

If your trade isn't in the list, you can still use QuoteMate — describe the job in plain English and it will build a priced quote.

## Trade-specific touches

Some trades get tailored help, for example:

- **Electricians** — RCD and safety-switch pricing, compliance notes on quotes.
- **Plumbers and gasfitters** — auto-suggested pipes, fittings and valves, hot-water-system pricing, and a live **Reece maX** integration for real trade-rate pricing (on by default for plumbing trades). See [Reece maX integration](../05-integrations/reece-max-integration.md).
- **Tilers and renovators** — waterproofing (AS 3740), tiles, screens, mixers and niches for shower and wet-area quotes.
