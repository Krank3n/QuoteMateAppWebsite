# Create your account and set up your business

You can create a QuoteMate account for free with no credit card. Download the app from the App Store or Google Play, or use the web app in any browser.

## Ways to sign up

- **Email and password** — sign up with your email address. You'll need to verify your email before you can use the app; until you do, you'll be signed out.
- **Google** — sign in with your Google account.
- **Apple** — sign in with Apple (available on iPhone and iPad, and on the web).

One account works across iPhone, iPad, Android, and web. Your data syncs automatically.

## Setting up your business (onboarding)

The first time you sign in, a short setup runs through six steps. You can skip the optional ones and finish them later in Settings.

1. **Company name** — the business name that appears on your quotes and invoices.
2. **Trade** — pick one or more trades so QuoteMate tailors templates and pricing to your work.
3. **Contact details** — your email, phone, and ABN (your ABN prints on every document).
4. **Branding** *(optional)* — upload your logo and pick a brand colour.
5. **Rates** *(optional)* — set a default labour rate and default markup so new quotes start with your numbers.
6. **Payments** — connect Square so you can take card payments and get paid.

Plumbers and gasfitters also get a step to connect **Reece maX** for live trade pricing.
