# Supported platforms, devices, and offline use

QuoteMate runs on iPhone and iPad, Android phones and tablets, and any web browser. One subscription covers all of them — there's no per-device charge — and your data syncs across every device you sign in on.

## Platforms

- **iOS** — iPhone and iPad (App Store).
- **Android** — phones and tablets (Google Play).
- **Web** — any modern browser at the web app.

## Works offline

QuoteMate is local-first. You can create and edit quotes on-site with no signal, and everything syncs automatically once you're back online. Your work is saved on the device and merged safely with the cloud so nothing gets clobbered.

## Device notes

- **Square Tap to Pay** (taking card payments on the device) needs an **iPhone XS or later on iOS 16.4+**, or an NFC-capable Android phone. See [Getting paid with Square](../03-invoicing-and-payments/getting-paid-with-square.md).
- **Push notifications** and in-device tap-to-pay are mobile features; the web app doesn't do these.
- The app uses a **dark theme**.
