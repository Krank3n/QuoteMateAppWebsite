# Quick start — send your first quote

Once your account is set up, you can send a professional quote in a couple of minutes. Here's the fastest path.

## Steps

1. **Start a new quote.** Tap **New Quote** on the dashboard.
2. **Describe the job.** Either pick a job template (deck, fence, pergola, stairs, and more) or describe the job in plain English — for example, *"Build a 4x3 metre hardwood deck with stairs."* You can also use voice-to-text on-site.
3. **Add the customer.** Enter the customer's name and details.
4. **Review the materials.** QuoteMate suggests the materials and quantities with live supplier pricing. Edit anything — add, remove, or change items and prices.
5. **Set labour and markup.** Add your labour hours (or days), your rate, and your markup. Add a travel adjustment if you're driving to site.
6. **Preview and send.** Check the branded PDF, then send it by email (with a pre-written message) or share it by SMS or WhatsApp.

When the customer accepts, convert the quote to an invoice in one tap — no re-entering anything.
