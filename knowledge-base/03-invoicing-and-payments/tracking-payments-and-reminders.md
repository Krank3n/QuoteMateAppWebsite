# Tracking payments and chasing unpaid invoices

QuoteMate keeps on top of who's paid and who hasn't, and can chase both unaccepted quotes and unpaid invoices for you.

## Payment tracking

- Every invoice shows a status: **draft, sent, paid, partial, or overdue**.
- **Overdue invoices are detected automatically** once they pass their due date.
- Record **partial payments and deposits**; QuoteMate tracks the balance owing.
- When a customer pays online, the invoice is marked **paid automatically**, a **receipt is emailed**, and the **GST is logged for your BAS**.

## Automated invoice reminders

QuoteMate can send reminders on unpaid invoices on a sensible cadence — for example a nudge **1 day before due**, then **1 day after**, **7 days**, and **14 days** overdue — so you're not the one sending awkward follow-ups.

## Automated quote follow-ups

For quotes that have gone quiet, QuoteMate can send follow-ups (around **day 3, 7, and 14**) to nudge customers who haven't accepted yet.
