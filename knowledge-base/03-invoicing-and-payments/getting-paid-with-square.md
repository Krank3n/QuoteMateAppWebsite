# Getting paid with Square

QuoteMate takes card payments through Square. You can tap a card or phone on-site with **Tap to Pay**, or send the customer an **online payment link** on the invoice. No card reader and no monthly hardware rental.

## Tap to Pay on-site

Tap a customer's card or phone straight on your device to get paid the moment the job's done — usually ready about 30 seconds after you finish. It accepts Visa, Mastercard, Amex, Apple Pay, and Google Pay.

**What you need:**

- An **iPhone XS or later on iOS 16.4+**, or an **NFC-capable Android** phone.
- A **Square account** connected to your ABN.
- The QuoteMate app.

Manual card key-in is also supported. See [Connecting Square](../05-integrations/square-payments-integration.md).

## Online payment links

Every invoice can carry an online payment link so the customer can pay by card from their phone or inbox. When they pay, the invoice is marked paid automatically, a receipt is emailed, and the GST is logged for your BAS.

## Fees

Two fees apply to a card payment: Square's own processing fee, and QuoteMate's platform fee. QuoteMate's platform fee **drops when you're on Pro**.

| | Free plan | Pro plan |
|---|---|---|
| **In-person (Tap to Pay)** — Square ~1.6% + QuoteMate | 1.7% | **1.5%** |
| **Online payment link** — Square fee + QuoteMate | 1.7% | **1.0%** |

As a rough guide, the **total cost of acceptance in-person on Pro is about 3.1%** (Square ~1.6% + QuoteMate 1.5%). Lowering the QuoteMate fee is one of the reasons tradies upgrade to Pro.

> On the **Free plan**, the 1.7% QuoteMate platform fee is added to the customer's bill. On **Pro**, you choose whether to absorb the fees or pass a surcharge on (below).

## Surcharge passthrough (optional)

Turn on **"Surcharge payment fees"** in your Square settings and QuoteMate adds a clearly-labelled **2.9%** to the customer's total, so you receive the full quoted amount. The surcharge is capped at 2.9% to stay under the ACCC's excessive-surcharge rules for realistic card types. With it off, the fees come out of your margin.

## Payouts

Funds usually clear to your bank the **next business day**. Same-day deposits are available from Square for an extra fee.

## Refunds

Refunds are handled through **Square**, not inside QuoteMate — there's no refund button in the app. Process the refund from your Square account. (Note: deposits may be non-refundable once work has started or materials are ordered, per your quote terms.)
