# Invoices and converting a quote to an invoice

When a customer accepts a quote, you don't re-enter anything — QuoteMate turns the quote into an invoice in one tap. You can also create an invoice from scratch.

## Convert a quote to an invoice

Open the job and tap **Convert to invoice**. QuoteMate creates the invoice from the accepted quote with all the line items carried across. This is safe to tap more than once — if an invoice already exists for that quote, it returns the existing one rather than creating a duplicate. The job timeline records the conversion.

## Create an invoice directly

You can also start a new invoice without a quote — it uses the same screens as quoting (job details, customer, line items, pricing) in invoice mode.

## Deposits and partial payments

- **Deposits** — request an upfront deposit before you start.
- **Partial payments** — record part-payments against an invoice; QuoteMate tracks the balance owing.

## Invoice statuses

Invoices move through clear statuses: **draft, sent, paid, partial, and overdue**. Overdue invoices are detected automatically so you can chase them. See [Tracking payments and reminders](tracking-payments-and-reminders.md).
