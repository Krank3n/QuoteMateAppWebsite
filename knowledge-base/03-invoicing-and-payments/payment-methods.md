# Payment methods your customers can use

QuoteMate supports the payment methods Australian tradies actually use. Some are available on every plan; the bank-based options are part of Pro.

## Available methods

- **Square card payment** — Tap to Pay on-site, or an online payment link on the invoice. *(All plans.)*
- **Card** — record a card payment. *(All plans.)*
- **Cash** — record a cash payment. *(All plans.)*
- **Cheque** — record a cheque payment. *(All plans.)*
- **Bank transfer** — show your bank details on the invoice. *(Pro.)*
- **PayID** — *(Pro.)*
- **BPAY** — *(Pro.)*
- **PayPal** — *(Pro.)*

On the **Free plan**, your customers pay online via Square. Bank transfer, PayID, BPAY, and PayPal are **Pro** options (included in your 14-day trial).

## How payment details appear

The payment options you enable show up on your invoice PDF and on the online payment page, so customers know exactly how to pay you.
