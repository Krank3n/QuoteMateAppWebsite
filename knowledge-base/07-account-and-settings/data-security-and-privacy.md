# Data security and privacy

Your business data is stored securely in the cloud and synced across your devices. QuoteMate is built on Google Firebase and complies with Australian privacy law.

## How your data is protected

- Stored on **Google Firebase**, with encryption **in transit** (TLS/SSL) and **at rest** (AES-256).
- Synced securely across the devices you sign in on.
- QuoteMate **does not sell or rent** your personal data, and doesn't share your business data with third parties for their own use.

## Privacy compliance

QuoteMate complies with the **Australian Privacy Act 1988 (Cth)** and the Australian Privacy Principles (APPs). You have the right to access, correct, delete, and port your data, and to complain to the OAIC.

## AI processing

When you use AI features, your job descriptions may be processed by third-party AI services to generate suggestions. No personal identifying information or banking details are sent for this.

## Deleting your account

You can delete your account in-app. When you do, your data is deleted or anonymised within **30 days**, in line with the Privacy Policy.

## What's collected

Typical data QuoteMate holds: your name, email, business name and ABN, phone, logo, and any payment or bank/PayID/BPAY details you choose to put on your invoices.

QuoteMate is not intended for anyone under 18.
