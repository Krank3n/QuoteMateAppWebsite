# Troubleshooting common issues

Quick fixes for the things tradies most often ask about.

## "No prices found" on a material

Usually the item name doesn't match how the supplier lists it, or you haven't saved a price for it yet. Try:

- Rewording the search to match the supplier's shelf name.
- Saving a **supplier favourite** for that item so it prices next time.
- Tapping **Fetch prices** again after editing the name.

More detail in [Live supplier pricing](../02-quoting/supplier-pricing.md).

## Can't log in / "verify your email"

If you signed up with email and password, you must **verify your email** before you can use the app — until you do, you'll be signed out. Check your inbox (and spam) for the verification email. If it hasn't arrived, try signing up again or contact support.

## My changes aren't syncing

QuoteMate works offline and syncs when you're back online. If something looks out of date:

- Check you have signal or Wi-Fi.
- Make sure you're signed in with the **same account** on each device.
- Give it a moment — changes merge automatically once you're connected.

## My free trial ended sooner than expected

The 14-day trial starts on your **first quote**, not at signup. If it feels short, it's likely the clock started when you created your first quote. See [Free trial](../06-pricing-and-billing/free-trial.md).

## Refunding a customer

Refunds are done in your **Square account**, not inside QuoteMate — there's no refund button in the app. See [Getting paid with Square](../03-invoicing-and-payments/getting-paid-with-square.md).

## Web vs mobile differences

A few features are mobile-only: **Square Tap to Pay** (needs the phone's hardware) and **push notifications**. Everything else — quoting, invoicing, jobs — works on the web app too.

## Contact support

Email **tom@hansendev.com.au**, or use the contact form on the QuoteMate website. Pro customers get **priority support**. There's no phone line or live chat.
