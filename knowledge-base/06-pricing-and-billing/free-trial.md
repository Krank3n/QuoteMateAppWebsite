# Free trial — how it works

Every new user gets a **14-day Pro trial** so you can use every feature before paying for anything.

## When the trial starts

The trial starts **when you create your first quote**, not when you sign up. That way the clock doesn't run down while you're still setting up your business details — you get the full 14 days from your first real quote.

## What you get during the trial

Everything on Pro: AI material and title generation, all PDF templates, your logo on documents, the extra payment options (bank transfer, PayID, BPAY, PayPal), the lower Square fee, and invoice and payment tracking.

## No credit card to sign up

You don't need a card to create your account or to start the trial. You only enter payment details if you choose to subscribe to Pro.

## What happens when it ends

When the 14 days are up, you move to the **Free plan** — you keep unlimited quotes and invoices and live pricing, but the Pro-only features switch off and the higher Square fee (1.7%) applies. Upgrade to Pro any time to turn everything back on.
