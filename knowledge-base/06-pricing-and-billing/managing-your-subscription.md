# Managing, upgrading, and cancelling your subscription

You manage your plan from **Settings → Subscription** in the app. How you're billed depends on where you signed up.

## Upgrading to Pro

Tap **Upgrade** in the app (or on the paywall). Choose monthly or annual and confirm. Pro features switch on straight away.

## Where you're billed

- **iPhone / iPad** — billed through your **Apple App Store** account.
- **Android** — billed through your **Google Play** account.
- **Web** — billed by card through **Stripe**.

Your subscription status syncs across all your devices, so upgrading on one shows up everywhere.

## Cancelling

You can cancel any time:

- **iPhone / iPad** — cancel in your **App Store** subscriptions.
- **Android** — cancel in your **Google Play** subscriptions.
- **Web** — cancel in the **Stripe customer portal** (from Settings → Subscription).

When you cancel, you keep Pro until the end of your current billing period, then move to the Free plan. Your data is preserved — nothing is deleted.

## Billing details

- Prices are in **AUD**, GST included where applicable.
- Subscriptions are **auto-renewing** and renew unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the period ends.
- One subscription covers all your devices; there's no per-device charge.
