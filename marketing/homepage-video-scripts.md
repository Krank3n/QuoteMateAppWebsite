# Homepage video scripts — mattress-factory series

Short (~15s) feature videos for the website homepage. Shot on a phone, in a
mate's mattress factory, no props beyond the phone (plus a bank card for the
payment ones and one sheet of paper for the bonus).

**Tone:** dry and deadpan, but every line still tells the viewer something real
about the app. The humour comes from the flat delivery and the fact you're
obviously in a mattress warehouse, not from filler reactions. If a line doesn't
teach the feature or sell the benefit, cut it. Fifteen seconds is enough for one
clear point, said well.

**Every claim here is a shipped, safe-to-say feature.** Don't add SMS reminders,
end-to-end voice-to-quote, calendar auto-sync, or refunds. Those aren't confirmed live.

---

## The creative rule

- Vertical (9:16), quick cuts, flat delivery. Not excited, just certain.
- Each clip makes ONE feature obvious: what it does, and why it saves time or money.
- Show the phone screen doing the actual thing. The demo is the ad.
- End cards (logo + line) go on in editing, not on camera.

---

## 1. THE PITCH *(hero — first on the page)*
**Covers:** quote on the job in ~2 min, not at home at night

- **[Dark warehouse. You lying on a mattress, laptop glow on your face, typing a quote at 9pm.]**
- VO (flat, worn out): *"This is when most tradies quote. After dinner. End of a twelve-hour day."*
- **[Sit up. Hold up phone.]** *"You don't have to."*
- *"Describe the job on-site, QuoteMate prices it off your suppliers' live rates, and sends a proper quote. Two minutes, before you leave the driveway."*
- **END CARD:** *Quote on the job. Not at night.*

## 2. TALK, DON'T TYPE
**Covers:** AI builds a full priced materials list from a plain-English description

- **[You near a mattress pile.]** *"I'll quote a whole deck in the time it takes to describe it."*
- **[Thumb a short line into the phone]** *"Four by three metre hardwood deck. With stairs."*
- **[Cut to phone screen: itemised materials list fills in with quantities and prices.]**
- *"Every board, every fixing, quantities and prices. That take-off used to be ten minutes on paper."*
- **END CARD:** *Describe the job. It builds the priced list.*

## 3. REAL PRICES
**Covers:** live supplier pricing, so you don't quote off stale numbers

- **[Leaning on a stack of mattresses like it's the timber aisle.]** *"Other apps price your job off a catalogue from whenever they built it."*
- **[Hold phone up]** *"QuoteMate checks your suppliers' actual prices today, as you quote."*
- *"Timber went up eight percent last week. So did my quotes. That's margin you'd have eaten."*
- **END CARD:** *Live supplier prices. Not last year's guess.*

## 4. GET THE DEPOSIT
**Covers:** take a deposit up front on the phone (deposits + Square Tap to Pay)

- VO: *"Plenty of tradies buy the materials, do the whole job, then chase the money for two months."*
- **[Tap a bank card against the back of the phone, card resting on a mattress.]** *"I take a deposit the second they say yes."*
- *"Tap the card on the phone. No reader, no hardware. Now their money's funding the job, not mine."*
- **END CARD:** *Deposit up front. Every time.*

## 5. TAP TO PAY
**Covers:** Square Tap to Pay on-site, money next business day *(bookends #4)*

- **[Dust your hands off on a mattress.]** *"Job's done, balance is due. Here's how I get paid."*
- **[Tap the card against the phone.]** *"Tap the card straight on the phone. Money's in the bank next morning."*
- *"No card reader, no invoice in the post, no waiting on 'I'll transfer you next week.'"*
- **END CARD:** *Tap to pay. Paid on the spot.*

## 6. WHERE'S THE CATCH
**Covers:** Free forever tier, and what Pro adds

- VO (flat): *"QuoteMate's free. Not free-trial free. Free free."*
- *"Unlimited quotes, unlimited invoices, live supplier pricing, card payments. Zero dollars a month."*
- *"Pay when you want the AI writing your quotes and your logo on every doc. Or don't."*
- **END CARD:** *Free to start. Pro when you're ready.*

## 7. BONUS — SNAP THE PRICE LIST *(needs one sheet of paper)*
**Covers:** photo/PDF supplier price-list import

- **[A supplier price list taped down the side of a mattress. Long.]** *"Supplier price list. Hundreds of items."*
- **[Point the phone camera at it. Snap.]** *"Photograph it, and QuoteMate reads every line into your prices."*
- *"No typing it all in of a night. Your rates, straight off the sheet."*
- **END CARD:** *Photograph the price list. It does the typing.*

## Filming cheat-sheet (phone only)

- **You can't film yourself hands-free** — get your mate (whose factory it is) to hold the phone, or lean it on a mattress stack.
- **Film the app screens separately** in tight close-up and cut them in. Don't try to show your face and the screen in one shot.
- **Shoot vertical**, use roller-door/skylight daylight, do **3 takes of every line**. The flat, certain read usually turns up on take 3.
- **Sound matters more than picture** — a factory echoes. Say lines close to the phone mic, or wear earphones with a mic.
- Cut in CapCut. Add logo + end-card line there.

## Accuracy notes

- Safe anchors: Free forever (unlimited quotes/invoices), Pro $49/mo or $328/yr,
  14-day trial that starts on your first quote, Square Tap to Pay (payout next
  business day), live supplier pricing, AI quote from a description,
  photo/PDF price-list import, deposits & one-tap quote to invoice.
- Don't name specific suppliers on camera or in end cards (Bunnings, Reece, etc.)
  without written permission — say "your suppliers" / "live supplier prices"
  instead. The real supplier names can appear naturally inside the app screen
  recordings, since that's the product's own UI, not a marketing claim.
- In #2, show the description being typed/thumbed in, not spoken end-to-end.
  AI-from-a-description is shipped; full voice-to-quote is not, so don't imply it.
- Keep off camera until confirmed live: SMS reminders, end-to-end voice-to-quote,
  Google Calendar auto-sync, Xero bulk sync/CSV, in-app refunds.
