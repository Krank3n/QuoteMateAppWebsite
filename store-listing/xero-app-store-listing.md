## About QuoteMate

QuoteMate is a quoting and invoicing app built for Australian tradies. Describe
a job in plain English and the app builds the materials list with quantities
and live pricing from major Australian suppliers. Add your labour and markup,
and send a branded PDF quote from the ute before you've left the driveway.

When the customer says yes, the quote becomes an invoice in one tap. Take card
payments on-site with Square Tap to Pay or send an online payment link. GST is
calculated automatically and your ABN sits on every document.

It's built for sole operators, small crews, and subbies across 24+ trades —
carpenters, sparkies, plumbers, builders, painters, landscapers, and more. One
subscription covers iPhone, iPad, Android, and web.

## QuoteMate + Xero

Invoices you raise in QuoteMate land in your Xero file without re-keying
anything.

- **Push invoices to Xero in one tap.** Every line item comes across at the
  prices on the invoice — your trade rates, not retail.
- **Contacts created automatically.** New customers in QuoteMate appear in
  Xero, so your bookkeeper isn't matching names by hand.
- **Bulk sync.** Behind on admin? Sync a batch of invoices at once.
- **CSV export.** Prefer to import manually, or your accountant wants a file?
  Export Xero-compatible CSVs any time.

Connecting takes a minute: **Settings → Xero Integration**, sign in with your
Xero login (secure OAuth — QuoteMate never sees your password), pick the Xero
organisation to sync with, done. Disconnect whenever you like.

## Key benefits

1. **Two-minute quotes.** Describe or photograph the job; the AI drafts the
   priced materials list. You check it, add labour, send.
2. **Live Australian supplier pricing.** Current prices, not last quarter's
   catalogue, plus your own price list.
3. **Paperwork that does itself.** Quote → invoice → Xero in three taps. GST,
   ABN, and branded PDFs handled.
4. **Get paid faster.** Square Tap to Pay on-site, or online payment links.
   Pro adds bank transfer, PayID, BPAY, and PayPal.
5. **Built for Australia.** AUD, GST, ABN, Australian suppliers. Nothing to
   configure.

## Getting started

1. Download QuoteMate from the App Store or Google Play (or use the web app).
2. Create your first quote — this starts your 14-day Pro trial, no credit card
   needed.
3. Go to **Settings → Xero Integration** and connect your Xero organisation.
4. Convert an accepted quote to an invoice and tap **Push to Xero**.

## Pricing (AUD)

- **Free** — $0 forever. Unlimited quotes and invoices, live supplier pricing,
  cloud sync, online payments via Square.
- **Pro** — $49/month or $328/year (≈$27/month). Lower Square fees, more
  payment options (bank transfer, PayID, BPAY, PayPal), AI material
  generation, all PDF templates, your logo on documents, priority support.
- 14-day free Pro trial for every new account.

## Submission notes

- Xero's review checks that the "QuoteMate + Xero" section describes the
  integration accurately — keep claims limited to what ships (invoice push,
  contact creation, bulk sync, CSV export). No "two-way sync" claims: the
  current integration pushes to Xero; it does not pull invoice status back.
- Xero certification requires OAuth 2.0 (already how the integration works)
  and a documented disconnection flow (exists: Settings → disconnect).
- Reviews on the Xero App Store weight heavily in marketplace search. After
  listing goes live, ask the handful of active Xero-connected users to leave
  one.
