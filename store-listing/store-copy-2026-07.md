## 4. App preview video (Apple) / promo video (Google Play)

**Apple rule (must follow):** App Previews must be genuine, freshly-captured footage of the actual submitted build running on-device — no live-action/lifestyle b-roll, no mockups, no footage of a different build. Text overlays and light background music are allowed on top of real screen capture. Length must be 15–30 seconds; script below targets ~20–22s.

**Google Play:** the promo video is a YouTube link and has no such restriction — lifestyle b-roll (tradie, ute, job site) mixed with real screen capture is fine and generally converts better here, as long as the app footage shown is genuine.
